Plan your visit

The market is held on the first and third Saturday of each month from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

Getting here

Use the east entrance for parking, bike racks, and accessible drop-off.

Before you arrive

  • Bring a refillable water bottle.
  • Check vendor updates before leaving home.
  • Bring small items for repair in a labeled bag.
